\paragraph{Version bridges.} A study run on an earlier version of a component may cite this paper for the methods if
the two versions agree. Table~\ref{tab:bridges} lists the agreements measured between the released components and their
earlier versions, on public ITP control cohorts and on the calibrator's reference fits. A study that ran an earlier
version reports its own bridge in the same way: it re-evaluates the log-likelihood of its stored best fits, at the
stored parameters and with the same data and observation model, under the release, and gives the largest difference
per individual. Such study-specific bridges belong to the studies themselves.
